Motion

Motion marks focus or a change of state, nothing else. Its durations and curves are matched to Steam's Gaming Mode where a clear match was found (Steam baseline), so moving into an Enamel app feels familiar, and are Enamel's choice otherwise (Enamel).

Use the Motion switch at the bottom of the menu to see the whole guide in reduced motion. Without a choice there, the guide follows the device's setting. The demos are shown side by side, so several look focused at once. In an app, only one element shows focus at a time.

Motion tokens

Every token in the Motion group, with a demo. Press Replay to run it again.

Focus

Move focus along a shelf of tiles and a list. A focused tile grows to --en-focus-scale over --en-motion-slow without moving its neighbours. Its ring appears --en-focus-ring-start thick and narrows over --en-motion-ring, then breathes --en-pulse-count times and rests fully visible, about five seconds after focus arrived. Each time focus arrives it all starts again. When focus leaves, the tile and ring return over --en-motion-fast. A row's colours fade over --en-motion-fast.

Lift and press

Only things you press move physically: buttons and picker rows. When focus arrives the face rises --en-lift over --en-motion-medium as its underside appears, and settles back over --en-motion-fast when focus leaves. Hold a press: it drops onto its underside over --en-motion-fast and darkens, and release springs it back over --en-motion-medium.

Picker, toast and action bar

Open each picker and close it with its Cancel row, Escape, or by choosing a row. The darkened, blurred page appears at once and the panel fades in over --en-motion-dialog; it leaves the same way. The focused row rises and presses as a button does. A toast slides in from the right over --en-motion-slow with --en-ease-enter; three seconds after its last update it fades out where it is, over the same time with the same curve, as Steam's own toast does. All of this is run by components/enamel-components.js, the same file apps use.

Reduced motion

The same focus demo, forced into reduced motion with data-en-motion="reduced" on this section. There is no breathing, no growing, no narrowing, no rising and no sliding: the ring simply appears, a focused button turns cream at once, a press only darkens, the colours still fade over --en-motion-fast, and focus ends up exactly as visible as in full motion.
