Resize

Enamel components adapt to the space they are given, never to a screen size, a device or an aspect ratio. Here each one sits in a frame: it responds to its frame, as it would to its space inside an app.

Several examples look focused at once, so each frame shows focus. In an app, only one element shows focus at a time.

Resize by hand

Drag the bottom right corner of a frame to make it narrower, wider, shorter or taller, and watch the component adapt.

Fixed widths

The same components in frames from very narrow to very wide. Scroll along the row. Each frame shows a screen with the action bar, the components and the hint bar; a picker over a screen; and a toast above the hint bar. The narrowest frame is the smallest supported box's width, 20rem; it also shows the picker and the toast over a hint bar whose hints, Select and Cancel, wrap onto two lines, and both still stop just above it. Widths are in rem, so a larger browser font makes the frames and everything in them larger; the pixel widths are at the browser's normal font size.
